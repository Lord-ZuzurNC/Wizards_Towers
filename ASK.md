## 1. The pitch

**1.1** Describe the game in one sentence, as if to a friend.
> That's a game where you play as a wizard and you buy villages and construct towers to grow you power and figh other wizards.
> That's multiplayers and you have a system where you construct yourself your big spells.

**1.2** Name 2–3 existing games it resembles, and what you take from each.
> Magicka: A system where you contruct your spells
> Mount & Blade Bannerlord: You play a character that walk across a map, can buy cities and upgrade them
> Vampire Survivor: Some map event that attack players, PvE, treasure on map etc, and a small rogue lite.

**1.3** What does the player *do* in a typical 10-minute stretch?
> In a 10min start a player will search for PvE to obtain EXP and Golds then buy a city to get a gold income and build a tower in it to build their mana income.

**1.4** What's the one thing that must feel good, or the game is dead?
> If a player die he get some Reincarnation Points that get them kind of passive perks for their next try.

## 3. The towers

**3.1** Is "tower" literal (a building the player owns/builds/climbs) or figurative?
> It is literal. Player build and own them, but don't climb.

**3.2** Does the player *build* the tower? If so: free placement, grid, room-based, or pre-authored floors?
> It is automatically placed in a village/city player just choose to biuld one in the city or not.

**3.3** Is the tower a base to return to, the whole playfield, or a dungeon to ascend/descend?
> It is a mana income for the player to create and cast more powerful spells. It is also a target to other player to conquer a village or weaken a player.

**3.4** Are there enemy/rival towers? Do they act on their own?
> Each player's tower is ennemy to other players. The tower self-defend depending on what a player chose to upgrade the tower.
> The Game (PvE) can also have towers.

## 4. The magic

**4.1** Where does magic come from — mana pool, reagents, time/cooldowns, risk/corruption, something else?
> Magic comes from: Mana is basic player's pool + Tower income. Magic have cooldowns and will get some risks by using it, including corrupt the player.

**4.2** How does the player get new spells: found, bought, crafted, researched, levelled into?
> Player can buy some very basic components on NPC villages but they can also get some components on Tower upgrades, by defeating some PvE bosses, and inside some rare treasures.

**4.3** Is spell *combination* a mechanic (mixing elements/components), or is each spell a fixed thing?
> A spell is a player-determined combination mechanic. The player have a limited spell slot and will mix components to create a spell in a free slot to be able to use it on the map.

**4.4** Roughly how many spells at launch: a handful (5–10), a set (~30), or a system that generates them?
> At launch there will be bunch or components with handful the game will show to player as a tuto then the player will have to use their imagination.

**4.5** Can the player fail badly — permadeath, lost tower, corrupted wizard? What's the worst outcome?
> Player can be defeated by another wizard's spell directly or by PvE. Loosing a tower will only remove all the upgrades they granted and the mana income. Spells that uses a lost element is locked and can't be casted.

- (b) **Personal run**: you die → your wizard is gone, you rejoin the *same* persistent world as a new wizard.
- (c) **Match**: small lobby (8–20 players), one map, 30–60 min, then everyone leaves.
