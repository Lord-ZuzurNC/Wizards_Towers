## The Collector in the sealed ROOF

The Collector is the only world boss inside the Monolith, and the only one nobody is told about.
Its stats and its fight are in `WORLD_BOSSES.md`; how it is met is here.

- A wizard who selects the ROOF while it is still sealed gets nothing the first and the second time.
- The third time, an ill omen appears on screen.
- The fourth time, the Collector pulls that wizard into the ROOF for a world boss fight.
- Any other wizard who selects the sealed ROOF four times joins the same fight.

The fight does not open the ROOF, is not the final battle, and never ends the run.
